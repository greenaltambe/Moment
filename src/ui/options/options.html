To enable Moment in Private Browsing, open your browser extension settings and toggle "Allow in private windows".

Private by design.

Moment is a local-first browser extension. Your Moment data is stored on your device and is not sent to Moment or a remote server.

Your Data

Local Storage Moment stores your rules, collections, settings, usage history, and temporary passes locally on your device.
No Telemetry Moment does not send analytics, telemetry, or usage information to remote servers.
No Tracking Moment does not use tracking pixels, advertising identifiers, or third-party analytics.
No Account Moment does not require an account, login, or cloud service.
Works Offline Moment's core functionality does not depend on an internet connection.

Our Philosophy

Moment is designed to interrupt automatic browsing and create space for conscious choice. Its interventions are intended to be mindful rather than punitive — without shame, dark patterns, or unbreakable traps.
